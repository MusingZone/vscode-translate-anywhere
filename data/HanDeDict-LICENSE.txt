Changes made: the Chinese -> German entries were reversed into a German ->
Chinese index (German glosses as keys; simplified Chinese headwords with
tone-marked pinyin as values), surnames and variant cross-references were
dropped, glosses were lower-cased and stripped of parenthetical notes, and
each key keeps a ranked selection of at most six Chinese words per part of
speech. The exact source snapshot (date and SHA-256) is recorded in
data/dictionary-de-source.json; tools/build-de.py reproduces the file.
